# Centerline Stroke Extraction & Geometric Text Noise Reduction

Started 2026-08-31 01h36. Planned; awaiting user approval.

## Context & Objectives

From testing on `cover.jpg` (and similar artwork/covers):
1. **Thin Ink Lines & Outlines**: Face contours and ink lines are currently traced as closed filled ribbon polygons (`<path fill="...">`), creating double-sided boundaries instead of a single stroke with width.
2. **Geometric Text & Antialiasing Noise**: Geometric text and sharp rectilinear elements inherit raster antialiasing wobbles and speckles from low-resolution or compressed source images.

**Goals**:
- Support **stroke extraction (centerline vectorization)**: identify thin ribbon regions and emit `<path d="..." stroke="..." stroke-width="w" fill="none"/>`.
- Implement **rectilinear & geometric edge snapping**: snap near-axis-aligned edges in text and geometric shapes to exact horizontal/vertical lines, eliminating raster staircase noise.

---

## Technical Approach

### 1. Centerline & Stroke Extraction

- **Ribbon Region Detection**:
  - Analyze regions with bounded thickness $w = \text{area} / \text{length}$.
  - Identify stroke topology: regions bounded by two long roughly-parallel boundaries and two short end caps.
- **Medial Axis / Centerline Extraction**:
  - Compute the skeleton/medial axis of the ribbon region (averaging opposite boundary curves or using distance transform topological skeleton).
  - Estimate stroke width $w(t)$ along the centerline.
- **SVG Stroke Emission**:
  - Fit splines/polygons to the centerline points.
  - Emit single-path elements with `stroke` and `stroke-width` attributes in SVG generation instead of filled loops.

### 2. Geometric Text & Noise Reduction

- **Axis-Aligned Snapping**:
  - For segments whose entry and exit points are nearly horizontal or vertical ($|\Delta y| < \epsilon$ or $|\Delta x| < \epsilon$), snap intermediate control points to exact horizontal (`h`) or vertical (`v`) alignment.
- **Sharp Corner Preservation**:
  - Ensure text glyph inner/outer corners ($90^\circ$ and sharp angles) are detected as hard nodes and not rounded by spline smoothing.
- **Antialiasing Fringe Removal**:
  - Enhance `--min-thickness` and `--filter-speckle` filtering around high-contrast text boundaries to absorb antialiasing halos into text/background.
